# 02 — Writing Samples: Prompts

> **Entity type:** `WritingSample` (corpus, role: prompt)
> **Source:** Raw user messages — Travis writing TO an AI agent.
> **Use:** Primary stylometric substrate. These are unedited Travis voice in his most natural register (informal-directive). Highest signal for diction, sentence rhythm, and lexical preferences.

The samples below preserve original capitalization, punctuation, and run-on structure. Annotations (in italics under each sample) flag stylistic signatures.

---

## Sample 1 — Initial ask (zed-workspace)

> i want to totally replace all my vscode based development tools with zed, and the most important part of that is workspace support that zed and all the other acp-driven ai tools will understand. that is the only feature that zed seems to be completely without

*Signatures:*
- Lowercase opening ("i want") even on a serious request
- Compound sentence joined with "and"
- Casual deictic closing ("that is the only feature...")
- Concrete and total: "totally replace all"
- Direct identification of the gap, not the wish ("the most important part of that is...")

- "etc." as casual closer in lists

- "I wanted to have them be..." — colloquial framing for a serious commercial intent
- Imperative-as-bullet mixed with declarative
- Geographic and relational anchoring ("in Mississippi", "the president of...")

## Voice Register (Two Modes)

Travis writes in two clearly distinguishable modes:

**Mode A — Casual Directive** (informal, fast, lowercase-friendly):
Used for: starting requests, redirects, quick corrections, mid-stream adjustments.
Example: *"i want to totally replace all my vscode based development tools with zed..."*

**Mode B — Architect Specification** (formal, structured, properly cased):
Used for: stakeholder-facing artifacts, brand documents, brief drafts, anything that will be read by Randy / Neil / Brittney / etc.
Example: *"Citizens National will have a named team — not a rotation of junior resources."*

The shift between modes is **context-dependent, not effort-dependent.** Travis is not "trying harder" in Mode B. Both are equally authentic. The substrate extractor must capture both.
