## Research-Informed Structure (from Tavily, both passes)

### Stylometry baseline (Eder 2017, DH conferences)
- 2,000-word minimum for reliable authorial attribution
- 5,000+ words is the historical conservative threshold
- Diversity of samples > raw volume; current substrate is ~22,000 words across registers

### TwinVoice (ACL ARR 2026) three-dimension framework
1. Social Persona — public interactions ✅
2. Interpersonal Persona — private dialogues ✅
3. Narrative Persona — role-based expression ✅

### SoulTrace methodology (Tavily-confirmed)
- Adaptive Bayesian inference (medical-diagnostic-quality methodology)
- 24 questions, 1–7 Likert scale, ~8 minutes
- 25 archetypes from 5-color probability distribution
- Built by ML engineers from Meta, Google, Hugging Face

### CliftonStrengths methodology (Tavily-confirmed)
- Developed by Gallup based on Don Clifton's decades of research
- 177 paired statements, ~30 minutes, 20 sec/response
- Formula: **Talent × Investment = Strength**
- 34 themes sorted into 4 domains: Executing, Influencing, Relationship Building, Strategic Thinking
- Top 5 = "Signature Themes"; Top 10 = strongest themes worth daily investment
- Over 90% of Fortune 500 companies have used the assessment
- **Categorical / ranked** (not probabilistic) — but Gallup acknowledges themes are talent patterns, not fixed identities
- **Critique:** focuses on positives without addressing weaknesses; limited external validation beyond strengths-philosophy
- Achiever is most globally common; Travis has Achiever at #4 — high but not dominant
